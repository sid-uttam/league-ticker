How it's worked out

Each manager gets a 0-100 score from three things, across every gameweek this season (11 so far): how many of the league picked the same captain (45%), how many of their 11 are in most other teams (45%), and points spent on hits (10%). The fewer people you copy, the more daring you are. 50 is your league's average manager. Ghosts (no transfers or chips in 3+ gameweeks) aren't rated.
