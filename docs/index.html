Straight answers

What it isn't

Another stats site

The numbers are there to back up a story. If something doesn't tell you anything new about your league, it doesn't make the page.

An app to download

It's a link your league opens from the group chat. No accounts, no passwords. You pick your name once and it's yours.

Made up

Every number comes from FPL's own data and is checked before it goes up. If a line doesn't match the numbers, it's rewritten or it doesn't run.
