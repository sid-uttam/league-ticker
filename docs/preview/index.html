How it's worked out

Each manager gets a 0-100 score from three things, across every gameweek this season (10 so far):

  • Captain (45%): on average, how many of the league picked the same captain as you. The fewer, the bolder.
  • Team (45%): how many of the 11 you pick are also in most other teams. The fewer, the bolder. We use the 11 you picked, not the one after auto-subs.
  • Hits (10%): points spent on extra transfers, compared with the league's biggest spender.

Making lots of free transfers doesn't count on its own: only who you pick and what you pay for it. 50 is your league's average manager; the further from 50, the further from the crowd. Ghosts (no transfers or chips in 3+ gameweeks) aren't rated and don't count towards the crowd.
